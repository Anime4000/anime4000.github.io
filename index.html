01 · xPON Freedom ／ 光回線に自由を

The box at the end of your fibre should be yours.

For most subscribers, fibre ends in a small white box the ISP hands over. You did not choose it, you cannot audit what runs on it, and when it fails you wait for a replacement.

The research began in 2020, the year the pandemic kept developers and hackers at home, and went public in 2021: documenting and reverse engineering Realtek RTL960x GPON sticks, so an SFP module in your own router can stand in for that box. What started as one repository is now a worldwide effort with its own firmware, its own tooling and a foundation behind it.
