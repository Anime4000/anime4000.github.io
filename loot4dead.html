Infected co-op

In normal Versus you take turns: one round as Survivors, one as Infected. On these servers you never have to be a Survivor. Four A.I. Survivors play the campaign from safe room to rescue, and every human player is a Special Infected trying to stop them. A reverse campaign.

The Steam group is called Lewd 4 Dead. I named it when I was younger, and it stuck — too many players know it by that name to change it now. Loot 4 Dead sounds the same, and it is what I call it here. Inside the code it has always been Noob4Dead.

History ／ 歴史

Part of Left 4 Dead 2 history

Left 4 Dead 2 came out in 2009. Most community servers from those years are gone. This one is not.

I have run these servers since January 2015, through a deleted Steam group, a fork and every SourceMod upgrade since.

In 2026 the YouTuber NasCoz reviewed the Steam group servers you find in Left 4 Dead 2, and opened with ours.

Playing ／ 遊び方

Finding a server

Join the Steam group, then open Left 4 Dead 2 and look under the group's servers. The live list, with the map and player count of every server, is on the SourceBans++ panel. It takes a few seconds to ask each server.

For the full experience, add your own custom player model and the Dramatic Epic Music addon.

The rumours ／ うわさ

No backdoor

A server this old collects drama. The claim I hear most is that my code has a backdoor. It does not.

The game mode is a few hundred lines of SourcePawn. My plugins do not give anyone admin rights and do not connect anywhere. The only server command they send is changelevel, to load the next campaign after a finale. Everything else on the server is well-known public plugins from AlliedModders, such as Silvers' left4dhooks.

Some years ago another server owner and I went separate ways. Their servers still run my old code under their own name; mine carry on as Lewd 4 Dead, with the original settings and rules.

Cheaters get banned for good. Type !report in game, or bring a screenshot or video to the Discord server. Appeals go through the SourceBans++ panel.
